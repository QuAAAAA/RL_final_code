\section{Problem Formulation}

We formulate the emotionally expressive and duration-controlled text-to-speech synthesis task as a fully observable Markov Decision Process (MDP). Within the IndexTTS2 framework, the environment is defined by the autoregressive Text-to-Semantic (T2S) generation process, where the agent (the language model policy $\pi_\theta$) generates a sequence of discrete semantic speech tokens token-by-token. At any step, the state $s$ encompasses the available conditioning information: the input text sequence, a speaker timbre embedding $c$, a duration constraint embedding $p$, and a target global emotion embedding $e$. The action $a$ represents the generated semantic token sequence, which is subsequently converted into a mel-spectrogram by the Semantic-to-Mel (S2M) flow-matching module.

To achieve fine-grained prosodic control without compromising speech naturalness, the RL learner receives a multi-objective scalar reward signal $R(s, a)$ upon generating a complete sequence. This feedback is composed of three task-specific components: an Emotion Classification Reward to ensure categorical accuracy, a Global Emotion Intensity Reward measured by the distance to a neutral centroid in the Valence-Arousal-Dominance (VAD) space, and a Local Emphasis Control Reward calculated from the maximum relative pitch ($f_{pitch}$) and energy ($f_{energy}$) of target emphasized words. 

The optimization problem seeks to maximize the expected reward using Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO). Specifically, the objective function aims to optimize the policy parameters $\theta$ by maximizing $\mathbb{E}_{s \sim \mathcal{D}, a \sim \pi_\theta} [ R(s, a) \nabla_\theta \log \pi_\theta(a|s) ]$, subject to a Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence constraint $\beta \text{KL}(\pi_\theta(\cdot|s) \| p_{\text{SFT}}(\cdot|s))$ to prevent catastrophic deviation from the base Supervised Fine-Tuned (SFT) model. We operate under the assumption that the discrete semantic token space produced by the T2S module is inherently expressive enough to implicitly discover and capture subtle emotional intensities and local emphasis variations prior to acoustic reconstruction.
